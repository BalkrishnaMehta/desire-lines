What did the models actually do?

          
Mapped informal=yes paths are useful labels, but incomplete ones.
          A supposed false positive may be the unmapped path we are trying to discover.
          Spatial folds keep nearby examples out of both training and test sets. TabPFN is
          license-gated; Gemma review runs locally on CPU through Ollama when available.
